- Distribute your contributions under the Project's current license (Apache License 2.0).

every contributor's work, which is impractical at scale. By signing once, you remove that

## How to sign

By submitting a pull request to the Project, **You agree to be bound by this CLA**.

For automation (when a CLA bot is wired into CI), You may also indicate agreement by:

- Adding `Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email>` to each commit (`git commit -s`), **or**
- Replying to the CLA-bot prompt on Your first pull request with `I have read the CLA
  Document and I hereby sign the CLA`.

If You are contributing on behalf of Your employer, please reach out to the maintainers to
execute a Corporate CLA before submitting.
